The honest version

Asking for reviews is awkward. So we stopped asking you to ask.

You're great at running a studio. Chasing parents for Google reviews is not the job you signed up for — but it's the difference between 4.6 and 4.9 stars in your market.

The old way

  • Mental note at pickup: "ask Maria for a review" — forgotten by Tuesday.
  • Bulk-email blast that lands in the spam folder.
  • Front-desk staff hand-copying parent emails into a Google form.
  • The cringe of asking the same family three times.
  • A one-star review from the one family having a bad week — answered three weeks late.

The pulsereview way

  • We watch your Jackrabbit classes for milestone moments — first cartwheel, level-up, recital.
  • An SMS goes out by name, ~2 hours after class — when the warm fuzzies are peak.
  • 5-star ratings get sent straight to Google. Anything lower goes to your private inbox first.
  • Dashboard tells you exactly what changed — and brings the receipts.
How it works

Three steps. We'll walk you through every one.

Set up a call, connect your data, pick your moments. We're hands-on with every new studio while we sharpen the product.

Connect Jackrabbit

Paste your Jackrabbit API key and we sync your families, classes, and instructors. Read-only — we never write back. Automation needs a data warehouse connection; we'll help you set it up.

Pick your moments

Choose the triggers that matter — class milestones, paid-in-full, 90-day anniversary, recital week. We handle the timing.

Reviews land. You reply.

Each new review pings your dashboard. Reply in your own voice — we handle the routing across Google, Yelp, Facebook, and the rest.

Features

Everything a studio needs. Nothing it doesn't.

Six things we obsess over, so you don't have to.

Smart timing

Requests go out ~2 hours after a great class — when the dopamine is still working in your favor.

SMS + email, your call

Pick the channel per family — and we honor each family's contact preferences right from Jackrabbit. If they've opted out of SMS or email, we skip that channel automatically.

Negative-review catcher

Anything under 3 stars routes to your private inbox before it touches Google. Save the relationship, then ask again later.

Pulse health dashboard

Live waveform of your reputation. Stars by location, by instructor, by week. Easy enough for the front desk; deep enough for the owner.

Multi-location ready

Run 3 gyms? 30? Each location keeps its own Google profile, its own brand voice, its own dashboard. One pulsereview login.

Jackrabbit native

The only review tool built around your class data.

Generic review tools blast everyone in a spreadsheet. We send the right ask to the right family because we understand your studio — not just a list of email addresses.

  • Knows your families. First names, student names, lesson history — all pulled live from Jackrabbit, never re-typed.
  • Knows your moments. First class. Level promotion. Recital. Renewal. Each one a perfect time to ask.
  • Knows when to wait. Missed payment, withdrawn class, cancelled session — we sit those families out automatically.
  • Read-only. We never write to your Jackrabbit data. Disconnect in one click.
