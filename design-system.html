The system behind pulsereview.

Review generation on autopilot — for businesses that want more 5-star reviews without the hustle. This is the brand foundation, the token library, and the kit of parts the product is built from.

Updated 2026-05-12 One self-contained file Geist · Bricolage · JetBrains Mono
01 · Identity

Brand & logo

The mark is a heartbeat waveform inside a 16px-radius squircle — confident, tech-forward, with a literal pulse. The wordmark is always lowercase. The brand is the one place pulsereview "shouts."

Mark, wordmark, inverse
Use the mark alone in tight spaces. The wordmark belongs at logo-lockup sizes (≥56px tall).
Rules
  • The brand name is always one word, all lowercase: pulsereview.
  • The mark uses a 16px squircle (rx=16 on a 64-square) — matches --radius-lg.
  • Never recolor the mark gradient. Inverse uses solid white on --pulse-600.
  • Minimum clear-space equals the height of the heartbeat path.

Rules of thumb
Six guardrails. If your copy passes all six, ship it.
You-first. Always second-person. "You'll get your first review within 24 hours."
We, the team. When pulsereview speaks, it's "we" — not "pulsereview."
Sentence case for everything: titles, buttons, menu items, headings.
Brevity beats cleverness. A pun is fine; a paragraph of puns is not.
Names, not nouns. "Maria from Sunshine Dental" beats "a verified customer."
Numbers when they help. "4.9★ across 213 reviews" — concrete beats filler.
